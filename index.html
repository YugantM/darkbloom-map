    Best advantage zones

    • Canada (Quebec / Manitoba) & Norway — cheap hydro, solid infrastructure
    • Turkey, China, India, Russia — low residential rates
    • Gulf countries (Qatar, Kuwait, Oman) — subsidised power
    • Some US states (WA, ID, LA) — competitive rates

    Highest cost zones

    • Ireland, Germany, Belgium, Italy, UK — $0.40+/kWh
    • Most of Western Europe and Australia
    • Island nations (Bermuda, Cayman, …)

    Why electricity matters so much

    A provider Mac draws only ~15–60 W. Monthly power cost ranges from under $1 in the cheapest countries to $8–15 in the most expensive ones — on an assumed $30–50 gross month, that can be the whole margin.

    Non-electricity factors

    • Wear & heat: sustained load shortens fan/battery life; laptops throttle headless
    • Demand volatility: utilization varies week to week — treat estimates as ranges
    • Privacy/security: you run third-party workloads on your machine; review what sandboxing exists before joining
    • Uptime bonus: always-on machines typically earn more calls

    How to start contributing compute

    1. Check your economics here. Use the map + calculator above. Net profit comfortably above zero? Continue.
    2. Pick the right machine. A dedicated Mac mini is ideal: low idle draw, no battery wear. Laptops work but watch thermals with the lid closed.
    3. Join the Darkbloom provider program. Install their provider client, connect your machine, and leave it online.
    4. Measure for ~a week. Compare Darkbloom's reported earnings with your electricity bill and adjust the rate above so the estimate matches reality.
    5. Re-evaluate monthly. Rates and demand shift — rerun the numbers before committing hardware long-term.
    Electricity prices are approximations of 2025–2026 residential averages (GlobalPetrolPrices, Eurostat, EIA) and vary by region/tariff. Earnings rates are editable placeholders, not official Darkbloom figures. This is an independent community tool, not affiliated with Darkbloom.
