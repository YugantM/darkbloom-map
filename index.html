Darkbloom Electricity Advantage Map

Residential electricity prices (USD/kWh). Lower prices = higher net profit when running a Mac as a Darkbloom provider.

< $0.08 (Excellent)
$0.08 – $0.15 (Very Good)
$0.15 – $0.25 (Average)
$0.25 – $0.35 (Expensive)
> $0.35 (Poor)

Best Advantage Zones

  • Canada (Quebec / Manitoba) & Norway – cheap hydro + excellent infrastructure
  • Turkey, China, India, Russia – very low rates
  • Gulf countries (Qatar, Kuwait, Oman) – heavily subsidised
  • Some US states (WA, ID, LA) – competitive rates

Highest Cost Zones

  • Ireland, Germany, Belgium, Italy, UK – $0.40+
  • Most of Western Europe and Australia
  • Island nations (Bermuda, Cayman, etc.)

Impact on Darkbloom

At ~15–30 W average draw, monthly power cost ranges from under $1 in the cheapest countries to $8–10 in the most expensive ones. On a $40–50 gross month, that difference is meaningful.

Data approximated from 2025–2026 residential averages (GlobalPetrolPrices, Eurostat, EIA). Hover over countries for exact values.
